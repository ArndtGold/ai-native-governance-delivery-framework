## Risks, Dependencies And Stop Conditions

- Protected/dirty scope: new baseline and staged/untracked preservation are mandatory. Unexpected foreign/history/approved-source modification or unsafe attribution stops completion and is resolved through existing owner.
- Missing runtime/dependency/browser capability: record the exact check failure and next owner action. No network install, global configuration or transferred proof. Native absence is the explicitly permitted limit, never a cover for missing required browser/protocol checks.
- Mandatory checks or reviews failed/missing: QA cannot cleanly pass. Run-approval response next-action text is not routing authority; after each approval fresh canonical dispatcher controls the next gate. No candidate-fix expansion is implicit.
