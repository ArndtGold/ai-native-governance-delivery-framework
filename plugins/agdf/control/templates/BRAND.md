# Brand and Design Intent

Complete this worksheet with confirmed project decisions. Declare the completed
profile in `SOT_REGISTRY.md` when it is the project's authoritative design source.

## Owner and scope

Name the accountable owner, the product, and the surfaces and audiences covered.

## Design intent

Describe what people should understand, feel, or be able to do when using the
product. Tie each priority to a concrete user task or situation.

## Information and visual priorities

Explain what deserves emphasis in relevant states and how the project balances
competing priorities. Record any confirmed constraints and their sources.

## Observable checks

For each applicable priority, name the scenario, the expected observable outcome,
and the evidence that would verify it. Record open decisions with their owners.

## Related sources

Link the project's design system, implementation values, and brand usage policy
where applicable.
