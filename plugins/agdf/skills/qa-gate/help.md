## NAME

`qa-gate` - QA gate

## SYNOPSIS

`/qa-gate` [`--help`|`-h`]

## DESCRIPTION

Makes the formal QA gate decision (`pass | revise | block`) based on TP coverage, Brownfield fit, solution integrity, evidence, and open blockers.

## EXAMPLES

Trigger the skill:

```text
> /qa-gate
```
